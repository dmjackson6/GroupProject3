PROJECT TUTWILER - HELP & INFORMATION

About Project Tutwiler

Project Tutwiler is a Cyberbiosecurity Vulnerability Triage System designed to help security professionals identify and prioritize vulnerabilities that could impact biological systems, healthcare infrastructure, pharmaceutical manufacturing, agricultural systems, and other biosecurity-critical sectors.

The system automatically:

  • Fetches vulnerability data from the National Vulnerability Database (NVD) and CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog
  • Uses AI to analyze each vulnerability for biological/biosecurity relevance
  • Scores vulnerabilities across multiple dimensions (Human Safety, Supply Chain, Exploitability, Patch Availability)
  • Generates actionable recommendations for addressing threats

Priority Levels Explained

CRITICAL

Composite Score: 70-100

Immediate action required. These vulnerabilities pose severe risks to biological systems, human safety, or critical infrastructure. They may affect medical devices, pharmaceutical systems, or agricultural technology with potential for widespread harm.

HIGH

Composite Score: 55-69

High priority threats that require prompt attention. These vulnerabilities could significantly impact biosecurity operations, supply chains, or safety-critical systems. Address within days to weeks.

MEDIUM

Composite Score: 35-54

Moderate risk vulnerabilities that should be scheduled for review. They may affect biological systems but with limited scope or impact. Plan remediation within weeks to months.

LOW

Composite Score: 0-34

Low priority vulnerabilities with minimal biosecurity impact. These are typically monitored but don't require immediate action. Review as part of regular maintenance cycles.

What Does "EXPLOITED" Mean?

A vulnerability marked as EXPLOITED means that:

  • The vulnerability has been actively used in real-world attacks
  • It appears in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog
  • There is evidence of malicious actors exploiting this vulnerability
  • Immediate patching or mitigation is strongly recommended

Exploited vulnerabilities are prioritized higher in the analysis queue and should be addressed urgently, even if their composite score is lower.

Understanding Scores

Each vulnerability receives scores from 0-100 in four key dimensions:

  • Human Safety Score: Potential impact on human health and safety (medical devices, patient data, etc.)
  • Supply Chain Score: Impact on biological supply chains (pharmaceutical manufacturing, food production, etc.)
  • Exploitability Score: How easily the vulnerability can be exploited by attackers
  • Patch Availability Score: Availability and ease of applying fixes or mitigations

These scores are combined into a Composite Score that determines the overall priority level.

How to Use the Dashboard

  1. View Statistics: Check the metric cards at the top for an overview of vulnerabilities by priority
  2. Filter Results: Use the priority filter, time range, or search box to find specific vulnerabilities
  3. Select a Vulnerability: Click any vulnerability card to see detailed analysis in the right panel
  4. Analyze Unanalyzed Items: Click "ANALYZE NOW" on unanalyzed vulnerabilities to run AI analysis
  5. Review Recommendations: Check the action recommendations section for specific steps to address threats
